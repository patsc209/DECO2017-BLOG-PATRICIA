I acknowledge the use of [Claude Sonnet 4.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) through the [Cursor](https://cursor.com) IDE for parts of this blog assessment.

**Where I used AI:** mainly the **look and feel** of the blog—not the weekly project story. I used Cursor to explore layout, typography, colour, and spacing in `assets/style.css` and related templates (for example font pairing, card styling, post image layout, and the Instagram-style carousel). I described the direction I wanted, reviewed each suggestion, and only kept changes I understood and agreed with.

**What I wrote myself:** all weekly post content, including reflections on SEAblings, functional requirements, technical choices (such as Leaflet and HTMX), testing, and scope decisions. Those ideas come from my work on the project with my team; I did not use AI to draft those posts.

**How I stayed in control:** for any CSS or small HTML the tools suggested, I read the diff before accepting it and could explain or change it myself. You can see what changed and when in the [GitHub commit history](https://github.com/patsc209/DECO2017-BLOG-PATRICIA/commits/main) for this repository.

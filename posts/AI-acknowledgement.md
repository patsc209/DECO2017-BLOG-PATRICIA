I acknowledge the use of [Claude Sonnet 4.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) (via the [Cursor](https://cursor.com) IDE) to support stylistic changes to this blog assessment. I used Cursor’s AI-assisted editing to explore layout, typography, colour, and spacing ideas in `assets/style.css` and related templates—for example font pairing, card styling, and the overall “cute” visual direction of the site. I prompted the tools with what I wanted the blog to feel like, reviewed every suggestion, and only kept changes that matched my intent; all weekly post content, project reflections, and technical decisions in the posts were written by and proofed with AI to make sure I was covering all the needed assesment requirements. Where the tools proposed CSS or small markup adjustments, I understood the changes before accepting them and could explain or modify them myself. All of this can be seen in the github repo commits page.
